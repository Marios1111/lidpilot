battery policy.

## RC4 matching helper and ten-minute performance

With the lid open and AC attached, the native RC4 panel started Keep Mac Running.
Independent read-back showed `SleepDisabled=1`, a LidPilot system-awake assertion,
no LidPilot display-awake assertion, and ServiceManagement's running helper at
parent bundle version 4. This proves a matching-build helper could acquire the
new lease after the signed upgrade.

The operator ran the same root-owned, read-only `proc_pid_rusage` V6 recorder
used for RC3, tracking GUI PID 19350 and helper PID 19359 for 600.005 seconds
from 2026-09-23 21:25:54 UTC. The popover was closed, lid open, AC attached,
and neither PID was replaced. All 121 samples completed. The report is retained
locally at `/private/tmp/lidpilot-keep-mac-running-rc4.json`, SHA-256
`b6dd7dd2399941a0d2441538a4381499380227534e8cf5d51fd9721e8367462a`.

RC4 CPU decomposes to about 0.102% GUI, 0.064% helper, and **0.519% reaped
helper children**. Those fixed `pmset` processes account for 75.8% of the
inclusive total. Removing the redundant renewal read improved the CPU result
by about 20% relative to RC3, but the budget remains missed by more than 3×.
The recorder's energy counter was 164,622,864 nJ; that is not Activity Monitor's
Energy Impact score. This one-host result is not a universal hardware benchmark.

The native panel then turned the session Off. Independent read-back showed
`SleepDisabled=0` and no LidPilot wake assertions; the build-4 helper remained
registered and running. Do not treat this as a passing V1 performance gate or
weaken state read-back, the watchdog, or recovery to make the number smaller.
