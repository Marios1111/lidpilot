# LidPilot launch film

The Remotion project in [`film/`](film/README.md) produces a caption-led
34-second LidPilot product film in landscape and vertical formats. Its native
panel source images are exact copies of the captures in `website/assets/`; they
remain unaltered. The views were staged in isolated Debug controls. That
provenance is recorded here rather than added as marketing copy or overlaid on
the images.

The film is product presentation, not hardware-validation evidence. Staging
provenance is recorded here rather than in its marketing captions. The project
includes generated WebVTT captions and a plain-text transcript. When the film is
embedded, keep playback user-started with no autoplay, `preload="none"`, and
pause it when the page is hidden.

Full-size masters and Remotion render/cache files are ignored. The 1280×720 web
encode, poster, captions, transcript, and source inputs are the deliverables to
review and track when rendered and approved.
