# LidPilot launch film

The Remotion project in [`film/`](film/README.md) produces a 42-second product
film in landscape and vertical formats. The story uses original vector laptop
animation and one ElevenLabs narration take, with a short visual lead-in and an
end-card hold. The film uses the owner's approved ElevenLabs take 1; final
playback review remains pending. The voice direction is young male, calm,
understated, and natural, with narration only.
Social compositions burn in synchronized narration captions; web video keeps
captions in a toggleable WebVTT track to avoid duplicate text.

The sequence is: a lecture screen dims; a local build's progress pauses as the
illustrated lid closes; LidPilot appears in its native panel; Keep Screen On
supports the reading scene; Keep Mac Running carries the task line forward with
the illustrated lid closed; Follow Lid connects the two; actual session
controls show the timer, Off state, and stop action; the film closes on the
free, open-source app and `lidpilot.app`. The task-line artwork is illustrative
and does not claim task detection or completion.

Native panel source images are exact copies of the captures in
`website/assets/`; keep them unaltered. The views were staged in isolated Debug
controls. This provenance is recorded here rather than added as marketing copy
or overlaid on the images. The film is product presentation, not
hardware-validation evidence. Its generated WebVTT captions and plain-text
transcript follow the timed narration. When embedded, keep playback user-started
with no autoplay, `preload="none"`, and pause it when the page is hidden.

Full-size masters and Remotion render/cache files are ignored. Review the new
masters, short preview, poster, captions, transcript, and source inputs before
updating the website or public deliverables. Preserve the existing
`Deliverables/LidPilot-Introduction-20260926` exports while the rewrite is under
review.
