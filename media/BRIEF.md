# LidPilot introduction — product film

Direction: precise native UI on a porcelain/graphite stage, slate-blue accents,
generous negative space, restrained moves and clean cuts. Original composition;
no borrowed product artwork, music, logo or animation. No decorative WebGL.

Runtime: 34 seconds at 30 fps. Master formats: 1920×1080 and 1080×1920.
Caption-led, with no unsolicited audio. A voiced version is optional, not a
requirement for this cut; no imitation or unlicensed stock audio.

Every native panel comes from LidPilot's controlled Debug preview with simulated
power controls. Product captures omit QA-only chrome; interactive QA previews
retain their notice. Record staging provenance in the source README rather than
in promotional scenes. This is product presentation, not hardware-validation
evidence. The evergreen end card shows the brand, domain and free/open-source
positioning, without release-status or blocker messaging. No claim of
immediate electrical panel shutdown, universal external-display support, idle
dimming independence, workload detection, or agent-completion automation.

Web delivery: user-started HTML video, preload none, efficient 1280×720 encode,
poster, captions, transcript, no autoplay and no hidden-page playback. Reduced
motion retains a static poster until explicit Play. Social exports keep large
legible captions within safe margins. Render caches and full-size exports are
ignored; only small web output and source inputs are tracked.
